About

Safety expertise, backed by an engineer's toolkit.

I'm an EHS professional — currently Assistant EHS Manager – Projects at Cushman & Wakefield — responsible for keeping people safe on active project sites. That means permits, risk assessments, inductions, inspections and the daily discipline that turns a busy build into a zero-injury record.

Alongside that, I'm a developer and ethical hacker with hands-on experience in machine learning and a keen interest in cybersecurity. My focus is building practical tools for my own field — most recently Guardian, an AI assistant for EHS teams. Knowing the safety problem first-hand is what lets me build software that actually solves it.
