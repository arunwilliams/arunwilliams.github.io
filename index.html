EHS · Projects

The person who signs off the site — and writes the tool that logs it.

I work in environment, health & safety, currently as Assistant EHS Manager – Projects at Cushman & Wakefield. My day job is keeping people safe on active project sites: permits, risk assessments, inductions, inspections, and the daily discipline that turns a busy build into a zero-injury record.

Alongside that, I build software. I'm a Python developer with a working knowledge of machine learning, computer vision and NLP — and a growing interest in security. The projects below range from image-recognition services to an AI assistant for EHS teams. The overlap is the point: I understand the safety problem well enough to code a real solution for it.
